## ⚠️ Scam awareness — fake domain renewal emails

**2026-08-27:** An email arrived from **zohobooks.com** saying the domain needed renewing.
**It was phishing.** Three things gave it away, and they're worth knowing for next time:

1. **Wrong company.** The domain and the email are both **Namecheap**. Zoho has no connection to
   this domain at all — and Zoho *Books* is accounting software that doesn't sell domains to anybody.
2. **Wrong date.** The domain doesn't expire until **March 2027**. A renewal notice arriving 18
   months early is a manufactured emergency.
3. **Unasked for.** Real renewal notices come from the company you actually pay.

Nothing was clicked and no password was entered, so no action was needed.
**Janet has confirmed she has no Zoho account of any kind** — so anything claiming to be Zoho,
about any subject, is junk and can be deleted on sight.

**Rules:**
- **Never click a link in a domain or email renewal message.** Type `namecheap.com` yourself, or
  open it from Dashlane, and check the account directly. This takes ten seconds and is always safe.
- **Expect more of these.** `.ca` ownership records are partly public, so scammers scrape them and
  send fake renewal notices in bulk. Knowing your registrar is Namecheap makes them obvious.
- **Losing a domain login is serious** — whoever holds it can redirect both the website and the
  email. It deserves the same care as the Shopify password.
